Accra Atelier

Built on heritage, not trends.

Kanae Rugs was born in Accra from a love for traditional West African textiles passed through generations of market traders. We elevate standard prints into structural, contemporary fits.

Every pattern choice honors the original lineage of the weave, cut carefully to minimize studio fabric waste while creating lasting statement pieces.

— Founder & Creative Director

Made-To-Order Process

Three smooth steps from consultation to your doorstep.

01

Consultation

Share measurements and garment preferences. Receive curated textile samples within two working days.

02

Tailoring & Fitting

03

Delivery
